# Create: Skyliners: the guide

Four ships to build, board and fly, with a page on the main features of each. The rest is there to be found aboard. The
[README](../README.md) has the pictures and the videos.

## The ships

- [The airliner](guide/airliner.md): an A380-style double-decker with a pool, a gym hall and a bar.
- [The zeppelin](guide/zeppelin.md): a Hindenburg-sized airship lifted by real hot air.
- [The rocket](guide/rocket.md): a rocket with stories to live in, who climbs into space and lands on her fins.
- [The freighter](guide/freighter.md): a battered saucer with guns, a club and a hyperspace jump.

## What you need

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.251 or newer, [Create](https://modrinth.com/mod/create) 6.0.10 or newer and
[Create Propulsion: Simulated](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-propulsion-simulated). To fly, also
[Create Aeronautics](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-aeronautics) and [Sable](https://modrinth.com/mod/sable); without
them the ships are buildings.

## Getting a ship

Everything is in the **Create: Skyliners** creative tab, the blueprints first; they have no recipe. Hold a blueprint and
aim at open ground: a see-through miniature shows which way she will stand. Use it to build her, sneaking to build
through whatever is in the way.

## What the ships share

- **The autopilot.** Each ship is flown mostly by her autopilot, from its control head aboard her. Set a speed, a
  heading and a height, or drop a pin on its map, and take off. Every control is a picture, a colour or a sound, so
  players who cannot read yet can fly too.
- **The airfield.** A runway sized for the airliner, a control tower, a launch pad and a jet bridge are in the creative
  tab too.

## Settings

The settings are under Mods → Create: Skyliners → Config; the server's open only while a world is loaded. Flying over
land nobody has been near can stutter while the server makes it, and generating the world beforehand, with a mod such
as Chunky, helps.
