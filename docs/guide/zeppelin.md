# The zeppelin

A rigid airship in the Hindenburg's style, half the real one's size: about 245 blocks long, her control car and decks
as big as ever. She is lifted by real hot air and flown from a glazed control car, mostly by her autopilot, and she has
decks to walk, a club to find and a roof to jump from. The full Hindenburg, 490 blocks, as long as three airliners nose
to tail, costs a lot to fly: the server setting `zeppelin.fullSize` brings back her full-size blueprints.

## Getting her flying

- Take a Half-Size Zeppelin Blueprint from the Create: Skyliners creative tab (the Furnished one also fits out the
  decks) and use it on the ground. The nose goes just past the block you click and the tail stretches away the way you
  face. She takes a few tens of seconds to go up.
- Her boarding stairs come down of their own accord. Her burners need Create Aeronautics.
- In the control car, use the autopilot's control head to open its settings and press Take Off.
- She is held a moment on her lines, then the handlers let go at the call "Up ship!" and she rises straight up, climbing
  on to the height you set.

## What to look for

- **The control car.** A ship's flight deck: wheels, engine telegraphs and a burner lever that move on their own to show
  what the autopilot is doing.
- **Liveries and flags.** Choose from six liveries and watch painters' cradles sweep the new paint over her. Hoist any
  banner at the flagstaff on top and her fins wear it.
- **Christening.** Swing a Christening Bottle at her nose and doves fly off, then her crest and name are painted on both
  flanks.
- **Decks and flank screens.** The furnished ship has a dining room, a bar and sleeping cabins. Great screens down her
  flanks show posters, your own map art or the faces of everyone aboard.
- **The Sky Deck.** Climb the rope in the Sky Shaft to the top of her crown. There are parachutes to jump with, paper
  planes to throw and a landing target to score them on.
- **Looking out.** The Lookout's Glass in the control car's lounge zooms in on the land below, and a mark you make
  there can become the autopilot's pin. Passenger screens show her outside cameras, including one that circles the
  whole ship.
- **Skywriting.** Press a button on the Skywriting Board and she draws a circle, a heart or a wave across the sky in
  coloured smoke.
- **A secret.** Somewhere along the gangway a hatch hides a techno club, with a floor that lights up to the beat.
