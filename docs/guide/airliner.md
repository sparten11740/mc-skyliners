# The airliner

She is an A380-style double-deck airliner, big enough to build in and walk about in while she flies. Furnished, she
carries a pool, a gym hall and a bar, and most of the flying is left to her autopilot.

## Getting her flying

- Take the Airliner Blueprint (an empty cabin) or the Furnished Airliner Blueprint from the Create: Skyliners creative
  tab. Use it on open ground: her nose goes just past the block you click and her tail stretches the way you face.
  The Runway Blueprint lays down a runway to take off from.
- She is built with her airstairs down. Walk up them, go forward and take the steps up to the flight deck.
- Sit in a pilot seat and use the autopilot's control head between the seats.
- Engage it. It has the Physics Assembler assemble her, folds the airstairs away and takes off.

## What to look for

- **The furnished cabin.** Business class, a lounge with a bar under a starlit ceiling, a duty-free kiosk and a second
  deck of galleries and seats above.
- **The moon pool.** A swimming pool sunk into the hold, with glass beneath it so you swim over the ground or clouds. A
  diving tower with a springboard stands at one end.
- **The gym hall.** Real basketball hoops, gymnastic rings, climbing ropes and wall bars. A ball still flies and
  bounces with the cabin in flight.
- **The flight deck.** Curved glass-cockpit screens, outside cameras and a calm voice calling out take-off and landing.
  Wear the pilot helmet for a head-up display.
- **Skydiving and drops.** Parachute racks stand by the aft doors. Open a door in flight and the cabin decompresses
  with a roar and drags you out. Care packages go out of the drop bay under their own parachutes.
- **Rough weather.** She runs into turbulence and air pockets, and lightning strikes her in a thunderstorm. She comes
  through unharmed.
- **Secrets.** A glowing book behind the bar opens a children's den with a ball pit and bunks. Five rubber ducks and
  three golden suitcases are hidden aboard.
- **Cabin life.** Villagers will ride as passengers and pay fares in emeralds, a duty-free counter sells paper planes
  and coffee, and trolleys roll down the aisle.
- **The big red button.** The self-destruct takes two presses. Everyone aboard floats clear under a parachute, and the
  explosion harms nobody.
