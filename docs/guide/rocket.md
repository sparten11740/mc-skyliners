# The rocket

She is a tall rocket on four fins, with a pointed nose and room to live in: stories stacked round a glass lift shaft,
and a cockpit in the nose. Her creative thrusters need no fuel, so she lifts off, climbs into space and comes back down
on her fins by herself.

## Getting her flying

- Use the Rocket Blueprint on the ground and she is built just past where you used it, her door facing you.
- Her airstairs come down by themselves once she is built. Walk up them and through her door.
- Ride the lift up to the cockpit and sit at the autopilot's screen between the commander's seat and the pilot's.
- Set a pin on its map, or just a height, and tell it to fly. She counts down ten seconds, then lifts off straight up.

## What to look for

- **Launch and landing.** Fire streams from under her, clouds billow over the ground and a shockwave throws back anyone
  standing near. She holds her height, leans toward her pin, then burns to land on it. Without a pin she comes back
  where she rose.
- **Launch control.** A voice on the radio counts her down and calls each milestone.
- **Space.** She climbs far past the top of the world. As she rises the sky deepens to black and the stars come out. The
  land becomes a hazy blue planet, shooting stars streak by, and northern lights ripple along its rim. Aboard you feel
  light for a while.
- **The lift.** It rides the glass shaft from the entry to the cockpit, its board showing each floor as a picture. While
  she flies it shows her height, with a little rocket climbing the sky.
- **Her rooms.** Every story is furnished as a room of its own: cargo bay, mess, bunk room, greenhouse, swimming pool,
  techno club, lab and, at the top, an observation deck under a starry dome.
- **The tardigrade.** The lab keeps a water bear the size of an elephant. Feed it moss through the hatch, or watch it
  sleep, burp and jump away through hyperspace.
- **The rover.** A six-wheeled rover waits in her cargo bay. Send it to the ground from the panel by its door, then
  drive it with a Rover Remote.
- **Cockpit controls.** A boost button kicks her engines up for a few seconds. A guarded stability switch lets her tip
  over, for the brave, and an abort handle scrubs or stops a flight.
- **Make her yours.** Pick one of six liveries at the livery selector.
