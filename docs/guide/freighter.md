# The freighter

A battered saucer freighter, a disc 60 blocks across with two prongs reaching forward either side of a loading gap. She
flies on thrusters that need no fuel, and she is as much a ship to explore as one to fly.

## Getting her flying

- Take the Freighter Blueprint from the Blueprints banner of the creative tab and use it on the ground. She is laid
  down just past the block you click, her two prongs toward you.
- Her ramp comes down by itself once she is built. Walk up it into her ring corridor, then through the tube from her
  front right room to the cockpit.
- The autopilot's control head stands between the pilot's seat and the co-pilot's. Set her to fly and she is assembled
  first, then lifts straight off on her thrusters.

## What to look for

- **The console.** Her cockpit desk has big buttons: one climbs her hard towards the edge of space, one slams her down
  to the ground (it only looks like a crash), and one jumps her through hyperspace to a pin set on the map.
- **Hyperspace.** Stars stretch into streaks for everyone aboard. Anyone outside sees her stretch and vanish in a white
  flash, and hears the thunder arrive late, as sound would.
- **Space weather.** High above the black of space, meteor showers, asteroid storms and solar flares blow through.
  Nobody aboard is hurt, however hard she is thrown. A solar flare's pulse briefly knocks out her lights, screens and
  engines.
- **Guns.** Climb the crew ladder to the gunner's seat for a laser cannon with rocket pods. Its bolts break space rocks
  block by block. The Laser Crossbow hangs on a rack in the entry hall.
- **The club.** A robot jazz trio plays on its stage and a robot bartender serves mocktails. A switch on the wall turns
  its gravity off, so everyone floats.
- **Her rooms.** Cargo hold, lounge, engineering and crew quarters are all furnished, and the lounge has a beach and a
  small pet tardigrade. Her computer talks to you in a gruff old voice.
- **The tractor beam.** Press the pad in the cargo hold and a green beam lifts whoever stands below up into her.
- **Running repairs.** She breaks down now and then in flight. Find the sparking panel or leaking pipe and whack it
  with the spanner.
- **Landing.** Drop a pin and she picks clear ground near it, as far as 200 blocks off, and comes down by herself. Her
  legs telescope to stand her level on a gentle slope; forest, water and steep ground turn her away, and she says so.
- **Stickers.** Each new kind of land she touches down on earns a sticker on the map in her entry hall.
- **Secrets.** The long tunnel down her starboard prong is a bowling lane, and a brass rivet in the wall at its end
  opens a smugglers' hold.
